## Current scope

The deliverable synthesizes complete timestamped journeys for five participant classes. It combines learned preferences over permitted route catalogs, a duration prior, a native-clock temporal diffusion model and road-coordinate decoding. The final G40 study adapts original spatial Hilbert–GASF/GADF/MTF and separate speed-image encoders with denoising, then samples codes from a training-only latent prior. No future reference GAF is used during synthesis; no image inversion or selected guidance timestep is claimed.

G40 is complete: 16 motion fits, 12 learned priors, eight new SUMO episodes, 128 generation/evaluation cells, 50,176 candidates and 30 downstream fits. All eight arms and five classes are included. Dual-view conditioning does not improve overall physical quality. Spatial-only augmentation has a small pooled gain, but opposite directions across the two evaluation OD cohorts. The manuscript retains these limits, the wrong-trip and raw-series controls, and failures.

The main results contain five complete-journey figures and five corresponding GAF panels, fixed by request order rather than score. Full native and coarse-clock metrics, coverage, per-seed/per-class tables, code interventions, utility cohort results and historical controls accompany them. The bibliography contains 54 substantively cited entries, including papers/preprints and explicitly identified thesis, repository and documentation sources; it is not described as 54 peer-reviewed papers.
