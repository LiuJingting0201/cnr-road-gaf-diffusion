# G14 — independent SUMO trajectory expansion
80 fresh Monaco MoST episodes with fixed split by complete simulation seed: 261001–261064 training, 261065–261072 validation and 261073–261080 reserved holdout. Source demand and SUMO settings match the G10 simulator: 20 target vehicles per motorized/bicycle category and 120 additional background passenger cars per episode. Keep at most one continuous central 512-point window per independent target vehicle after the existing movement, path and reconstruction checks. No per-class quota after screening. The five-column CSV uses episode-local SUMO seconds, SUMO XY in metres and original class names. The spelling bycicle is the source label. These are newly simulated ground-truth observations, separate from the group's two historical tables and from diffusion outputs. Do not mix old evaluation episodes into these files. Do not use G14 holdout for training or tune against the original opened test. Export and validate integrity, not holdout performance. Seed independence does not ensure disjoint streets or new-city transfer. No scheduled bus service or pedestrian simulation.

The optional full-trajectory archive exports all recorded target-vehicle FCD observations, including those excluded from strict windows. It omits background vehicles. Every strict window must match the same vehicle's full observations exactly in time and XY. Archives overlap by design; do not count their vehicles twice. Full tracks end at arrival or the simulation cutoff. Treat both datasets as observations of the specified SUMO demand and network, not as additional collected CNR drives.

## Reproduction
Use the G10 SUMO 1.28.0 environment and MoST network, in an isolated work directory. Provide the repository scripts and the existing src, .venv, g10-env and data locations. Run `bash scripts/g14_data.sh`, followed by `PYTHONPATH=g10-env:src .venv/bin/python scripts/g14_full.py g14-data`. The first command simulates episodes with up to eight CPU workers, then checks unique IDs, coordinate hashes, number of vehicles screened, split sizes, demand and FCD hashes, and CSV row counts. The second cross-checks the strict subset against the full FCD positions and validates the resulting full archive. An episode manifest alone marks simulation completion; packaged delivery requires both completion receipts. Input network, demand and FCD SHA256 hashes are in the strict ZIP's manifest. The files retain simulator-time seconds within each independent episode. The original main generator was trained on 425 strict windows; its published results pertain to that training set.
