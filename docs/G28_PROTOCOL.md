# G28 · Paired spatial and speed representations for trajectory diffusion

## Research question and authorization
The user's 2026-11-06 request now explicitly permits comparing both GAF representations on the same participant-class task. This supersedes the G23 rule restricting the speed CNN to six motion modes for this new experiment only. The original spatial branch continues to consume ordered XY only. The speed branch consumes speed derived from XY and timestamps only. Speed classification and spatial classification keep separate names. All teachers are our own models.

A plausible hypothesis is that each representation preserves useful class and within-class variation. Classification information alone cannot establish additional benefit conditional on an already supplied route and class. In particular, speed requires timestamps, while native spatial indexing and GAF resizing can lose physical duration. The deterministic transformation creates no new information. This experiment tests the usefulness of the representation and supervision.

G22 spatial training also added prescribed endpoint positions while later raw-reference evaluation omitted them. Use raw native-cadence observed XY consistently in fresh teacher training. Audit the existing raw-to-progress-to-route decoder on train/monitor trips before attributing representation failures to the diffusion network. Preserve all historical results with their definitions.

## Stage A, bounded teachers and reconstruction audit
Use 6,033 complete training trips and 1,126 monitor trips, with whole episodes separated. Record support failures and use the common valid IDs for matched representation comparisons. Train spatial original Hilbert/GASF/GADF/MTF and speed GASF/GADF/MTF ResNet18 readers for the same eight participant labels. Both start from the same ImageNet weights and seed-specific head initialization, 18 epochs, seeds 42 and 43, batch 64, identical optimizer schedule, monitor macro F1 selects epoch. Record raw speeds derived from timestamps, inherited spatial bounds, image resolution and speed scale. No hyperparameter sweep.

Freeze seed-42 teachers for generation training. Reserve seed-43 readers for secondary checks. Report full confusion matrices and per-class recall, eight-class and six-motorized macro F1. A route/location nuisance readout and raw-physics readout contextualize classification. Independent simulation episodes do not establish geographic generalization. SUMO vehicle defaults and route permissions can make some labels intrinsically overlapping.

## Stage B, directly supervise the noisy denoiser's hidden representation
All arms use the original continuous complete-trip G22 duration/progress target, 12,000 updates, seeds 42/43, class-balanced common batches and common initial denoiser weights. No explicit six-mode condition in this comparison. Add two identical small projections from the midpoint residual block's globally pooled hidden state into the frozen 512-dimensional teacher spaces. Globally centre and unit-normalize targets using training statistics. Frozen true-trip features appear only as training loss targets, never inference inputs. Weight cosine alignment by alpha-bar and a fixed coefficient 0.1. In the dual arm average the two branch losses so adding a view does not double the coefficient.

Six arms use identical projections and initialization. B uses denoising alone. P aligns to class-centroid targets in both views. S aligns to the same trip's spatial feature. V aligns to its speed feature. SV averages paired spatial/speed alignment. SH uses both features from a fixed other trip of the same class and route-length quartile. Log the shuffle's self-match coverage. This control preserves coarse class/length information while disrupting the within-trip target. P checks generic label supervision. S and V test incremental view contribution. No gradient through discrete Hilbert encoding is required.

## Stage C, independent synthesis evaluation
Generate SUMO seeds 279001–279008 with the fixed same-map protocol and retain the first six admitted requests per class/episode, selected by ID without reading movement. Sample four trajectories per model seed and request with the same noise across arms. Keep all candidates. Generate no ranking-based selection. Record failure/exclusion counts for simulation and encoding.

Primary contrast SV versus B. Co-primary outcomes are class-macro W1 of total duration and longest steady-moving interval. Report both, paired whole-episode 95% bootstrap intervals, no composite score or claim based on cherry-picking one. Supporting outcomes include four-second speed change, acceleration, stopping fraction, upper-tail speed, pairwise within-request speed-profile diversity, duration variability, clipping, endpoint consistency and lane-graph legality. Report six motorized classes separately. Additional contrasts S/V/P/SH establish mechanism and are exploratory. Require gains in physical quality without a collapse of diversity or map validity before claiming better synthesis. Recognizer confidence is a secondary diagnostic, and the teaching model never judges its own success as a primary endpoint.

Primary intervals condition on the two trained seeds. Two seeds cannot characterize broad training variance. Previous tests and G15 heldout remain untouched. Eight new traffic episodes share the training map. Store complete small metrics and negative outcomes. Stop after these prespecified arms. A staged failure or inability to complete is reported explicitly, not converted into evidence of efficacy.

## Literature connection
REPA (Yu et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06940) motivates noisy-denoiser alignment to frozen clean representations. Its image results supply a mechanism to test in trajectories. GAF (Wang and Oates, https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.00327) motivates time-series image representations. Neither establishes the effectiveness of the present two-view road-conditioned generator.

Interpretation notes fixed before outcomes. Monitor probes share the teacher checkpoint-selection partition and are developmental. Fresh references supply independent classifier checks.
