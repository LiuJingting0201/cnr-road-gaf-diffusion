# Full-trip synthesis replaces a fixed-duration window as the main deliverable
Predeclare legal complete routes on MoST before simulation. Random vehicle routes use explicit permitted directed lane successors (750–4,500 m target, accept connected chain within specified band); pedestrians use complete single-sidewalk trips for this first version and have limited support. Generate eight classes in mixed SUMO traffic. Record departures/actual arrivals separately from the simulation cutoff. Reject unfinished trips, discontinuities and endpoint mismatches, with coverage. Episode split already fixed: 16 train, 3 monitor and 4 evaluation; no frozen tests. No validation reference/timing supplied to generator. Simulation emits observed positions at one second with a 0.25 s internal step. Model whole itinerary duration jointly with a positive, normalized progress profile over relative time. The internal number of profile bins is a numerical representation, not an output duration; exported coordinates every quarter-second to the generated arrival. Evaluate endpoints, TTC here meaning trip travel time (avoid collision terminology), duration distributions, motion patterns, road adherence; first pilot is conditional on a complete planned route, not arbitrary urban walking or destination-free routing. Train a separate original Hilbert/GAF CNN on complete observed trips and assess it on complete generated candidates. Keep short-window CNN work as a distinct diagnostic.

Full-trip models use 256 normalized-time progress increments and one log travel-time residual jointly denoised, two seeds, 12,000 updates. The residual is relative to route length divided by a class-median training speed; at sampling neither duration nor observed future is supplied. Export departure-relative times every 0.25 s plus exact generated arrival. Inputs include both prescribed endpoints and a full directed route sampled before SUMO traffic. Compare against timing transferred from a random same-class training trip among 32 nearest route lengths; adjust by the ratio of prescribed lengths. Report distribution of travel time, per-request variation, caps and endpoint errors; road validity is structural on the admitted route and not evidence of speed realism. Fit separate complete-reference CNNs (original images vs motion angular fields) with episode-disjoint monitoring. For the horizon comparison fit whole vs central short original images only on common eligible IDs with at least 127.75 s; 128 one-second FCD observations cover 127 s, close to the earlier window, with different sampling cadence. For feedback select weights by monitoring only, disclose class coverage and compare equal candidate budget. Do not provide a test vehicle's realized future path to the generator.

Additional native-cadence check: replay the same arrived G22 participants at 0.25 s. Transfer the improved short-window temporal CNN to whole trips by processing every disjoint block, including both endpoints and the last block. Combine block logits using their represented duration; no cropped segment is used as a substitute for the trip. Fine-tune on complete-trip labels and select on the separate monitor episodes. Use this as an additional classifier for full-trip inference and compare against the original whole-trip GAF image model. Report selection/ablation explicitly. This is a supplementary whole-trip recognition design, not part of the original fixed-duration G17/G19 evaluations.
