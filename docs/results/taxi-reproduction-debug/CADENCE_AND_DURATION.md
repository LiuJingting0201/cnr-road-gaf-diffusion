# SUMO taxi cadence and duration controls

The starting cohort has 10,000 training journeys recorded every second. A 120-point cutoff on that clock covers 119 seconds. On Porto's 15-second clock the same cutoff covers 1,785 seconds. These two experiments measure different consequences of the observation protocol.

1. Keep the same 10,000 training journeys and 1,000 development requests. Take every fifteenth observation and retain the final observation, including a shorter terminal interval when necessary. Preserve departure time, elapsed duration and endpoint cells in the one-second training grid. Recompute distances and observation-count features. Reuse the two completed one-second models and retrain the fifteen-second representation with seeds 42 and 43. Score both sets of draws against identical fifteen-second references, grid boundaries and length bins. Also report scores under each native observation clock.
2. Create independent, completed SUMO taxi journeys with at least 120 regular fifteen-second observations. Use separate simulation episodes for training, development and holdout. Select 10,000/1,000/1,000 journeys with duration-bin proportions from the matched Porto training subset. The simulated demand uses intermediate waypoints to produce long journeys on the MoST network. This changes the route mix as well as the duration distribution. Compare both seeds with the matched 10,000-training-trip Porto runs on 1,000 held-out journeys per city.
3. Both new arms use the existing conditional coordinate DiffTraj with 200 points, 200 epochs and batch 220. Each run has 9,200 optimizer updates. Measure density JS, joint OD JS, length JS and top-20 hotspot F1. In the cadence arm also report physical length errors and per-request endpoint hits.

Server work directories: `/workspace/cnr-experiments/taxi-cadence-v1` and `/workspace/cnr-experiments/taxi-long15-v1`. The report watcher writes `CADENCE_REPORT.md` first and `COMBINED_REPORT.md` after both arms complete. A launch does not supply a metric or establish agreement with Porto.
