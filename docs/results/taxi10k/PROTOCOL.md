# DiffTraj on 10,000 SUMO taxi journeys

Train on the archived taxi IDs only. Recover all native 1-second positions, recompute coordinate statistics, eight headers, 16x16 grid and 20 distance-quantile bins from the taxi training population. Use the archived episode partitions (10,000 / 1,000 / 1,000). Conditional coordinate U-Net, no class embedding or auxiliary speed loss. Original mean trip speed remains a scalar header. Train seeds 42 and 43 for 200 epochs with constant-rate AdamW 0.0002, batch 220 over two GPUs per fit (9,200 updates), final weights and common sampling seed. Measure density JS, joint OD JS, length JS and top-20 hotspot F1 on all 1,000 test journeys. Include independent real/real control and physical distances. Pair with previously archived Porto long-trip fits, showing published Table 5 and equal-N 50-subset medians. Porto fits used 46,470 real training journeys, batch 1,024, clipping, annealing and best development checkpoints. Simulated demand, city, native cadence and scoring bounds also differ.
