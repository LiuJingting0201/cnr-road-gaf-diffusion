# Porto minimum-length comparison

DiffTraj Appendix B.1 describes Chengdu and Xi'an and says “For all datasets” when specifying removal below 120 observations. Section 5.1 points separately to Appendix C.1 for Porto. C.1 gives Table 5 and omits the retained population, eligibility rule, split and length-histogram edges. In issue 26 the reproducer explicitly describes a 120-point minimum. The author says the preprocessing is broadly similar and suggests lowering the cutoff. Issue 31 mentions no Porto-specific parameters. Neither reply verifies Table 5's actual training rows.

Sources: [paper B.1/C.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.11582), [issue 26](https://github.com/Yasoz/DiffTraj/issues/26#issuecomment-3056290687), [issue 31](https://github.com/Yasoz/DiffTraj/issues/31#issuecomment-3631139887).

Two original conditional DiffTraj models. Long arm uses all 46,470 complete training records of at least 120 points under the established taxi partition. Mixed arm draws 46,470 records uniformly without replacement from 1,391,486 complete training records with at least two coordinates, using reservoir seed 20261112. Two points are necessary to define motion and positive elapsed time. Input size is 200 after interpolation in both arms. Seed, 200 epochs, number of updates, optimizer, global batch 1024 and fast four-GPU microbatch 256 are matched. Each fits coordinate/header normalization on its own training set. This tests the training population at fixed compute; it does not train on all 1.39 million eligible trips.

Each fit is scored on the same two held-out taxi cohorts: 3,000 long requests drawn from the previous long holdout; and a uniform reservoir of 3,000 mixed requests from 125,702 eligible held-out trips. Test overlap between cohorts is allowed. No request's taxi belongs to either training population. Each cohort uses identical request ordering, original point counts, initial noise and sampler settings in both arms. Compute length JS in both shared 20-quantile bin systems (long-training and mixed-training edges). Compare within cohort and fixed edges, alongside metre-valued W1, signed distance bias and paired length ratio for moving trips. The local length metric and split qualify comparisons with the published 0.0242. A closer score cannot determine the authors' historical preprocessing.
