# Data-scale statement in DiffTraj issue 26

In a discussion of filtering and resampling the Porto data, the repository owner, Yasoz, suggested trying a lower minimum trajectory length and counting how many trajectories would remain. He described a dataset on the order of 100,000 trajectories as generally helpful for training. His Chinese wording uses “10w级别”. This is practical advice about the scale of a dataset. The issue supplies no per-class minimum or guarantee of performance. [Maintainer's comment, 10 July 2025](https://github.com/Yasoz/DiffTraj/issues/26#issuecomment-3056290687)
