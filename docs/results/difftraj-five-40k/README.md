# Five-class SUMO training expansion

The 40,000-per-class collection extends the verified native-clock 20,000-per-class release. The five training strata are bus, motorcycle, passenger car, bicycle and taxi. The target is 100,000 new one-second complete training journeys and 200,000 training journeys in total. The original 1,000 development and 1,000 test journeys per class keep their identities. Full native time, position, speed, class and arrival evidence are retained, alongside the 200-point coordinates and eight DiffTraj request attributes. The model-ready normalization is fitted on the expanded training set.

The finite server queue uses 12 parallel SUMO processes, new training episode seeds starting at 5,100,000 and a maximum of 3,400 episodes. Requests are allocated according to the remaining class counts. The original MoST demand generator, route and vehicle settings, and one-second eligibility rules are reused. The quota unit is a complete vehicle journey. The new training trips use native simulation departure times. Geometry fingerprints exclude exact 200-point duplicates across partitions. Historical raw shards are linked into the new release. Only new selected traces and corresponding arrival records are archived; large temporary FCD outputs are removed as episodes are packed.
