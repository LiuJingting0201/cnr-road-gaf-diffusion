# SUMO data inventory for DiffTraj

| Dataset | What a record represents | Appropriate experiment |
|---|---|---|
| Supplied midimost export | One observed vehicle track within the supplied simulation window. 1,478 training, 198 development, 177 reserved after the baseline vehicle filters. | Source-domain comparison against the supplied observations. Complete departure/arrival is not attested for every record. |
| G15 balanced eight-class archive | 8,000 training windows per class; one 512-point (127.75-second) window per entity. Road annotations are separately archived. | Fixed-horizon motion or road adherence. It cannot supply whole-trip duration or OD targets without returning to a separate full-trip source. |
| G30/G33/G35 public native-demand runs | G35 has 29,895 training legs from 24,073 parents and 7,230 development legs from 5,842 other parents, including 1,538 taxi legs and 685 bus legs in training. Earlier pilots and downstream evaluations may reuse source populations. | Separate native-demand validation or a domain-transfer experiment. Preserve parent and episode splits, filter short/truncated legs, encode planar coordinates and derive headers. Do not count these as extra independent complete-trip IDs or pool the historical experiments by summing their totals. |
| Five-class 10k release | 10,000 complete, eligible, separately identified training journeys per class and 1,000 per class in each held-out partition. Times at 1 s, planar x/y, exact episode IDs. | Ready for single-class or pooled DiffTraj. Refit normalization and grid on the chosen training subset using raw episodes. |
| Five-class 20k release | Nested enlargement of the previous training split. Reuse previously unselected training-episode tracks and supplement with fresh, disjoint, bicycle-enriched simulation episodes. | Same frozen validation/test IDs and same raw observations. Normalization is fit to the enlarged training population. For a data-size ablation use common evaluation grids and bins across models. |

The 10k and 20k releases overlap by design. Bus service geometry and stop order come from the native routes; schedules are synthetic. Taxi demand uses the SUMO taxi class and synthetic reachable OD requests, without dispatch orders. Five classes share the network, not the original CNR demand. New native trajectory shards live alongside the 20k pack and reused shards remain under the 10k root. Resolve raw records by episode prefix. Do not delete the 10k raw shards. The G35 job directories contain related project material and recovery intermediates. Size alone does not establish that their contents can be removed.
